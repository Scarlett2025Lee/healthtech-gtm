# Health-Tech Partnership Ecosystem & GTM Strategy

A portfolio strategy project exploring partnerships as an acquisition channel for a maternal-health startup serving pregnant and postpartum users.

The deliverable is an Excel workbook: a researched partner universe, prioritization model, segment-specific partnership playbook, outreach and discovery framework, and proposed funnel and pilot metrics.

## Business question

Which partner segments offer the strongest combination of relevant audiences, trusted referral opportunities, and a practical path to a lightweight pilot?

## Prioritization method

Candidates receive 1–5 ratings across six dimensions:

| Criterion | Weight |
|---|---:|
| Audience overlap | 25% |
| Referral potential | 20% |
| Strategic fit | 20% |
| Ease of activation | 15% |
| Trust | 10% |
| Expected acquisition impact | 10% |

Weighted score = 20 × sum(rating × weight), giving a score from 20 to 100.

Priority tiers: **High ≥ 88**, **Medium ≥ 78 and < 88**, **Long-term < 78**.

Scores are analyst hypotheses for prioritization, not measured partner performance or externally verified ratings. Expected acquisition impact is a judgment input, not an observed outcome.

## Partnership strategy

The workbook explores clinical referrals, reciprocal referrals, education, directory placement, co-marketing, and employer partnerships. Candidate segments include OB-GYN practices, fertility clinics, doulas, lactation networks, pelvic-floor/postpartum physical therapy, employers, and parenting brands.

The discovery framework tests partner needs, current referral workflows, decision processes, and willingness to participate in a pilot before committing to a broader rollout.

## Measurement plan

The proposed funnel tracks identification, prioritization, contact, replies, discovery, qualification, activation, referrals, and user conversion. The workbook also defines an illustrative four-week provider pilot, including exposure, click, signup, and activation targets.

**Funnel volumes, conversion assumptions, pilot targets, and decision rules are planning scenarios. They are not results of executed outreach or a completed pilot.** Candidate organizations listed in the workbook are prospects, not confirmed partners.

## Scope and limitations

This is a strategy and research artifact. Source URLs are included for follow-up; current organization details and contacts should be checked before outreach. The project does not demonstrate customer acquisition, revenue, clinical outcomes, or signed partnerships.

The original workbook is preserved unchanged. Its Interview Story tab contains suggested presentation wording; claims about research ownership or execution should be checked against actual contributions before reuse.
